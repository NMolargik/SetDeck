## Description — 3107/4000
```
Plan your week. Log every set. See your progress.
SetDeck is a strength training tracker built around the way lifters actually train: a routine for each day of the week, the exercises in it, and the sets inside each exercise.
Your data syncs privately through iCloud, and your Apple Watch is ready at the rack.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• A routine for each day, Sunday through Saturday, shown as a deck of exercise cards
• Visual exercise cards with muscle-group colours and icons
• Four set types: reps, AMAP (as many as possible), timed duration and freeform
• Mark exercises as warm-ups, reorder them, and set targets for weight, reps and time
• On supported devices, muscle groups can be suggested for you, on-device

LOG IN A TAP
• Mark sets complete as you go and rate effort with RPE
• Every completed set is saved to your history
• Switch between pounds and kilograms

SEE WHAT'S WORKING
• Training volume over time, with 30-day, 90-day and all-time views
• A muscle heatmap, front and back, showing what you trained this week
• Muscle distribution, balance analysis and intensity (RPE) charts
• Personal records with estimated one-rep max, plus progress for each exercise
• 19 achievements across consistency, volume, routine, variety and strength, celebrated with confetti

APPLE HEALTH, WATER AND ENERGY
• Record strength training workouts to Apple Health
• A Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island shows your workout time
• Log water and calories, and review hydration and energy charts
• Water and energy widgets for your Home Screen
• A Control Center control to start and stop your workout
• Share a summary of a finished workout

WATER REMINDERS (NEW)
• Optional reminders you switch on yourself, off by default
• Choose active hours, how often you'd like a nudge and your daily goal
• Reminders stop for the day once you reach your goal
• Tap "Log a Glass" right from the notification

BUILT FOR iPHONE DUO (NEW)
• Unfold and the routine deck sits beside your week at a glance
• Stats become a two-column dashboard
• Settings split into a list and a detail pane
• Everything still works beautifully on a standard iPhone and on iPad

REDESIGNED ROUTINE EDITOR (NEW)
• See your whole day at a glance
• Rich exercise cards with muscle-group color and icon
• Set types and targets readable without opening each row
• Clear controls to add, reorder and delete

APPLE WATCH
• See today's routine and log sets from your wrist
• A guided rest timer between sets
• Start and stop workouts from your watch
• A complication for today's workout
• Log your next set with Double Tap or the Action Button

SIRI, SHORTCUTS AND SPOTLIGHT
• Ask "What's my workout today?" or about any day of the week
• Start and stop a workout, check your status, add water or calories
• Your exercises are searchable in Spotlight

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your routines and history stay in your private iCloud
• Health data is read and written only with your permission
• No analytics or tracking

Available in English, Spanish, French (Canadian) and Japanese.

SetDeck is made by Farside Software. Questions or ideas? Find help and contact us at farsidesoftware.com/support.
```
